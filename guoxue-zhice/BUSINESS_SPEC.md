# Guoxue Zhice — Business Content Contract

- Knowledge scope: at least 20 classical works with source notes, methods, scenarios and explicit boundaries.
- Disputed authorship/textual status must be surfaced rather than hidden.
- Goal analysis must ask factual questions before applying classics.
- Analysis must use multiple classical perspectives when relevant.
- Every result must contain 5 Why and reversal/counterexample prompts.
- Output status remains ACTION_HYPOTHESIS; classics are not automatic truth.
- Review records connect prior judgment, action, result and lesson.
- Health-related classical material is historical context only.
- 涉及健康、养生、睡眠、饮食、身体或疾病相关问题时，古代文本只作为历史思想与文化背景材料，不替代现代医学，也不替代专业医疗评估、诊断或治疗。
- If a health-related situation may require clinical evaluation, the system must explicitly direct the user toward modern medical assessment rather than presenting a classical method as validated medical advice.
